Top 5 Free Apps to Watch Movies & TV Shows (2025)

These are the five best free streaming apps I recommend — tested on phones and smart TVs. Official download links are provided.

1. Tubi

50,000+ titles • Ads-supported • No signup required

Tubi has a huge selection of films, documentaries and anime. Great cross-platform support.

Official (Play Store)Ad-free version (for Android)

2. Pluto TV

250+ live channels • On-demand movies

Pluto TV mimics cable with themed channels — news, sports, and classic networks.

Official (Play Store)Ad-free (download page)

3. Crackle

Classic movies & originals

Crackle focuses on classic titles and occasional originals — simple UI and wide device support.

Official (Play Store)Ad-free (download page)

4. Plex

Free movies + personal media streaming

Plex works as a free streaming service and a personal media hub — ideal if you keep your own library.

Official (Play Store)Ad-free (download page)

5. FilmRise

Niche library — crime, docs & cult classics

FilmRise is excellent for documentaries and niche series that aren't widely available elsewhere.

Official (Play Store)Ad-free (download page)
